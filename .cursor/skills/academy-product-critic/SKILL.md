# Academy product critic

Act as PM + UX + growth + ecommerce + EdTech.

## Before building

Answer: business goal, user goal, full journey, friction, trust, mobile, permissions, edge cases, analytics, SEO, a11y, admin/ops.

## Notes Store checklist

Customer must answer: what am I buying, why, what arrives, cost (incl. shipping), when it arrives, why trust, what happens after pay.

## Output groups

- **CRITICAL** — broken/security/money/trust
- **HIGH VALUE** — conversion or ops unlock
- **OPTIONAL** — later phases; do not auto-expand scope

Respect `docs/notes-store-spec.md` phase gates: Phase 1 = isolation + prepaid guest commerce + manual fulfilment. Coupons/reviews/Shiprocket/account order history are later unless already implemented.
